## Lead funnel (implemented)
ZIP/country → homeowner → bill slider → interest → shade → credit band → timeline → address/property → name/email → phone + preferred contact + TCPA-style consent. Auto-advance on single-choice steps, progress bar, UTM/gclid/referrer capture, lead score 0–100, consent timestamp + page captured. Renters routed to portable/community solar.

Legal note: the FCC one-to-one consent rule was vacated (11th Cir., Jan 2025); TCPA prior express written consent still applies — keep the unchecked consent box, name buyers where possible, and store proof (e.g. TrustedForm).

## Monetization stack
AdSense (consent-gated slots) · pay-per-lead / exclusive leads to installers · featured installer listings · affiliate (power stations, panels, EV chargers) · sponsored guides, calculator sponsorship, newsletter & video sponsorship · contest sponsorship · donations (PayPal) & tiers · YouTube channel.
